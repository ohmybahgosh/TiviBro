# TiviBro icon and logo brief

Colors match the app UI.  Send back the 1024px icon to generate 80x80 `icon.png`, 130x130 `largeIcon.png` and a splash screen.

Design a brand identity for "TiviBro," an IPTV player app for LG smart TVs. The name combines "IP" (internet TV) with "tally," the red light on a broadcast camera that glows when that camera is live on air. The brand should feel like professional broadcast equipment: confident, precise, calm, made for a dark living room, not flashy or cartoonish.

Deliverable 1: App icon (symbol only, no text).
A square icon built around a single bold idea: a glowing tally light. Combine a solid circular "on air" light with a subtle signal or stream element, for example two or three concentric arcs radiating from the light like a broadcast signal, or the light sitting at the top corner of a minimal rounded TV screen outline. Flat vector style with at most one soft glow on the light itself. No gradients across the background, no photorealism, no 3D bevels, no drop shadows, no letters.
- Background: deep blue-black, hex #121A24, full bleed to the square edges.
- Main light: warm signal red, hex #E5484D, with a soft outer glow.
- Accent: amber, hex #F2B33D, used sparingly for the signal arcs or a thin highlight.
- Optional neutral: off-white, hex #E8ECF1, for a screen outline if used.
- It must read clearly at 80 by 80 pixels on a TV launcher seen from 10 feet away.  Strong silhouette, thick strokes, generous padding (keep the artwork inside the central 75 percent).
- Produce it at 1024 by 1024 pixels so it can be scaled down cleanly.

Deliverable 2: Horizontal logo (wordmark plus symbol).
The same tally-light symbol on the left, followed by the word "TiviBro" on the right.  Typeface: a modern geometric or grotesque sans serif, bold weight, slightly tight letter spacing, like Inter, Manrope or SF Pro Display.  Set "IP" in amber #F2B33D and "Tally" in off-white #E8ECF1, so the IPTV meaning is visible at a glance.  The red light should sit near the cap height so it reads as the "on air" signal.  Provide it on the dark #121A24 background and on a transparent background, at 2400 by 800 pixels.

Deliverable 3: Monochrome version.
The symbol and the wordmark in solid off-white on transparent, for use over video.

Avoid: play-button triangles, generic TV-with-antennas clip art, film reels, satellite dishes, the words "webOS," "LG" or "TV" in the artwork, purple or neon cyberpunk palettes, and any resemblance to TiviMate, IPTV Smarters or Netflix branding.
