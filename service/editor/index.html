3. Public guide fill-in

Channels that steps 1 and 2 don't cover can get a guide from two free, community-run guides: epg.pw and iptv-epg.org. Matched by guide id, a local station's call sign or the name. Your sources and your provider always come first. TiviBro hosts nothing and sends no logins: the TV downloads the files from them, at most twice a day. Learn more

Channel names

Strip clutter like "USA | " or " FHD" from every name at once. Channels you renamed keep your names.

Rules change how names look on the TV; the provider's names are never touched, so turning a rule off brings the old name back. The preview on the right updates as you type. Learn more

Your own prefixes and suffixes

Comma separated, like TiviMate. Spaces count: " HD" will not touch "WHD". Upper and lower case do not matter.

Backup and restore

Save everything you set up, and put it back on this TV or a new one.

A backup holds everything: settings, every playlist with its login, groups, favorites, names, guide matches and restore points. It is locked with a passphrase you choose, so the file is useless to anyone without it. To undo recent edits instead, use Changes and restore points. Learn more

Download a backup

Choose a passphrase to lock the file. You need it again to restore. Write it down or keep it in a password manager: nobody, us included, can open the file without it.

Chrome on a phone may say the file "can't be downloaded securely". That is because this page comes from your TV over your home network (http), not from the internet. The file is locked with your passphrase either way: choose Keep.

Restore a backup

Replaces the TV's settings and playlists with the ones in the file, logins included, and restarts TiviBro on the TV. Works on a brand-new TV too.
