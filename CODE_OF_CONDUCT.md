# Code of conduct

TiviBro is a small, friendly project.  Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to webOS, rooting or IPTV.
- Assume good intent, and discuss ideas, not people.
- Keep it on topic.  No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal details, and never share logins, playlists or stream links.
- Stay legal: TiviBro is a player for subscriptions you have the right to use.  Requests for or links to pirated content are removed.

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow these rules.  To report a problem privately, use the Security tab's "Report a vulnerability" form (it reaches the maintainer only) and say it's a conduct report.
