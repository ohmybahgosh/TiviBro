*On the TV*

**OK** (or **Hold OK > Remind me**) on a future programme in the guide or in search.  When it starts, TiviBro asks **Watch** or **Dismiss**, or switches by itself if you set **When a reminder starts** that way.  You can be reminded a few minutes early.

Reminders work with TiviBro closed too, as long as the TV is on: the TV shows its own alert over whatever you're watching, another app or an HDMI input included, and **Watch** opens TiviBro on the channel.  If it started a while ago and the channel has catch-up, TiviBro offers **Watch from the start**.  A reminder that came due while you were away is never lost: TiviBro asks when you come back, and one that already ended is offered from catch-up.  (Some TVs show a toast instead of an alert; select it to watch.)  Timing and behaviour are under [Settings: Guide, start screen, reminders](Settings-Guide-start-screen-reminders), which also lists your reminders (**OK** removes one).
