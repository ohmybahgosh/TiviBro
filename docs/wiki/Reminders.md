*On the TV*

**OK** (or **Hold OK > Remind me**) on a future programme in the guide or in search.  When it starts, TiviBro asks **Watch** or **Dismiss**, or switches by itself if you set **When a reminder starts** that way.  You can be reminded a few minutes early.

Reminders work with TiviBro closed too, as long as the TV is on: the TV shows its own alert over whatever you're watching, another app or an HDMI input included, and **Watch** opens TiviBro on the channel.  If it started a while ago and the channel has catch-up, TiviBro offers **Watch from the start**.  A reminder that came due while you were away is never lost: TiviBro asks when you come back, and one that already ended is offered from catch-up.  (Some TVs show a toast instead of an alert; select it to watch.)

Reminders are for someone using the TV.  When the screensaver, the Gallery or art screen, or Always Ready is showing, the alert waits and comes up once you're back (while the programme is still on); otherwise TiviBro asks the next time you open it.  Inside TiviBro, a reminder waits if nobody has pressed a button for 10 minutes and nothing is playing.  Timing and behaviour are under [Settings: Guide, start screen, reminders](Settings-Guide-start-screen-reminders), which also lists your reminders (**OK** removes one).
