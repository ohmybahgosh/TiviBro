*On the TV*

With your own free TMDB token saved (Settings > API keys), or without one from TheTVDB (see [No TMDB token?](No-TMDB-token-TheTVDB)), TiviBro finds the movies in your guide:

- **In the guide:** a movie's details show its poster, and a line with the year, runtime, genres and TMDB rating.  When the guide says little about it, TMDB's plot is shown instead.  Movies in the grid say **Movie** next to their time.
- **The Movies on now group,** near the top of the guide: the movies on now, most time left first, then the ones starting within the hour.  With none, it says so.
- **In the phone app:** an **On now** tab with the same posters and genre shelves; tap one for its backdrop, title logo, plot and channels (watch on the TV, or a reminder for one starting later).  Movies in the phone's guide and schedules show their poster.
- **The Movies on now screen,** in the side menu while a token is saved: big posters of the movies on now and starting in the next three hours, the one you're on large at the top over its backdrop, with its title logo (or its title when TMDB has no logo), its channel, time left, year, genres, rating and plot.  A movie on several channels is there once.  Each poster says whether it's on: the guide's **LIVE** badge and a progress bar for one playing now, or how soon it starts ("IN 35 MIN", or the time when it's more than an hour off) on a quieter poster for one starting later, and each shelf says what's in it under its name ("9 movies, join in progress", "3 playing now, 2 starting soon").  Under **Playing now** and **Starting soon** come the same movies by genre (playing now first in each) (**Action**, **Comedy**, **Family**…): a shelf for each genre with two or more of them, the biggest first, up to ten.  **OK** watches it fullscreen (Back comes back to Movies on now, on the same movie); for one starting later, **OK** asks whether to set a reminder or watch the channel now (the cursor starts on **Cancel**, so a stray press sets nothing).  **Hold OK** or **Red** lists the channels showing it, soonest first, with a reminder on any of them, or the guide.  A movie on more than five channels shows the first four and **All N showings…** for the rest.

Which programmes are movies: the guide's own **Movie** category, a year in the title ("Bridesmaids (2011)") or "Movie:", anything 75 minutes or longer on a movie channel (HBO, Cinemax, Starz, TCM, a "Movies" group…), and other programmes 80 minutes to 4 hours long that aren't news, sport or a show.  TMDB has the last word: a movie of exactly that title (and year), and for the last kind also well known and not a TV series of the same name.  Only the programme titles (and years) go to TMDB, with your token.  Answers are kept on the TV for 30 days, so after the first day only a few titles an hour are looked up.  Turn it off with **Movie details from TMDB**, or just the group with **Show Movies on now** (Settings > Channels and groups).
