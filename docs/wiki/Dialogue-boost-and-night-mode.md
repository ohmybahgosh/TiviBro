*On the TV*

Voices clearer over music and effects, and loud scenes no louder than talk, free (Plex has this with Plex Pass).  While something plays, open **OK > Dialogue boost**, or set it in **Settings > Audio and subtitles > Dialogue**.

- **Dialogue boost:** Off, Low or High.  What both speakers share, where voices are mixed (and where a surround track's centre channel goes), is lifted, the low boom is trimmed and the voice band is brought forward.  High does more of each and narrows the stereo a little, on purpose.
- **Night mode:** loud scenes brought down and quiet talk brought up, for watching with the volume low.  It works with or without dialogue boost.
- Both are off until you turn them on.  The TV plays sound in its own hardware, so TiviBro changes it before it gets there: while either one is on, the sound goes through the [audio converter](Sound-the-TV-cant-play) and comes back as stereo Dolby Digital, which every LG TV plays.  The converter downloads the first time it's needed.
- While it's on, a channel takes a second or two longer to start, the rewind buffer isn't used (the converter reads the channel itself), and sound that normally goes straight to a soundbar (passthrough) arrives as stereo.  Turn both off to get all of that back.  Changing it while something plays starts it again with the new setting.
