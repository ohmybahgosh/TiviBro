*On the TV*

**Sports** in the menu shows today's games with their scores and the teams' logos, from ESPN, and which of your channels has each one.

- **My teams** comes first: the games of the teams you follow this week, on now first.  Then **Live now**, every game being played, then a row for each league (today's games, or tomorrow's when it has none today).
- Each card has the two teams with their records (and rankings in college), the score and the clock, or when it starts, and the channel it's on.  A game none of your channels has says so, with the network that shows it.
- Channels come from your guide (a programme naming the two teams around the start, event channels and league passes included, the way My teams finds them) and from the national sports networks by name (ESPN, FS1, NFL Network, NHL Network and others).
- **OK** watches the game on its best channel.  A game more than five minutes away asks first: a reminder, or the channel now.  **Hold OK** lists every channel with it.  **Yellow** sets a reminder, **Green** follows one of the two teams, **Blue** picks the leagues: NFL, college football, NBA, college basketball, WNBA, NHL, MLB, MLS, the Premier League, the Champions League and Formula 1.
- Scores update every 30 seconds while a game is live, and only while the screen is open.  Turn the screen off in **Settings > Channels and groups > My teams > Sports in the menu**.
