*On the TV*

**Sports** in the menu shows today's games with their scores and the teams' logos, from ESPN, and which of your channels has each one.

- **My teams** comes first: the games of the teams you follow this week, on now first.  Then **Live now**, every game being played, then a row for each league (today's games, or tomorrow's when it has none today).
- Each card has the two teams with their records (and in college, a ranked team's poll rank, like **#4**; the game's page says **Ranked #4**), the score and the clock, or when it starts, and the channel it's on.  A game none of your channels has says so, with the network that shows it.
- Channels come from your guide (a programme naming the two teams around the start, event channels and league passes included, the way My teams finds them) and from the national sports networks by name (ESPN, FS1, NFL Network, NHL Network and others).
- **OK** (or **Red**, or **Info**) opens the game's page (below).  **Hold OK** asks: a reminder, for a game that hasn't started, or watch it now on one of your channels that has it.  **Yellow** sets a reminder, **Green** follows one of the two teams, **Blue** picks the leagues: NFL, college football, NBA, college basketball, WNBA, NHL, MLB, MLS, the Premier League, the Champions League, Formula 1 and cricket.
- **The game's page**: the score in the two teams' colours, then **Watch on** your best channel, **Remind me**, **Follow**, and **Hide** or **Show** the score.  Under them the box score by quarter, period or inning with each team's leaders, every channel of yours that has the game (▼ to them, OK watches), and the standings of the teams' division or conference.  **Back** from a game you watched from its page comes back to the page, and Back from the page to the same game on Sports.
- **Cricket** has the internationals (Tests, ODIs and T20Is, men's and women's) and the big T20 leagues (IPL, Big Bash, PSL, The Hundred, SA20, ILT20, CPL and others) on now, just played and next.  The score is written as in cricket (**245/6 (42.3 ov)**) and the line under it says how the match stands (**India trail by 297 runs**, **New Zealand won by 6 wickets**).  It's shown at first on TVs set to India, Pakistan, the UK, Australia and other cricket countries; elsewhere turn it on with **Blue**.  A match is found in your guide (a programme naming both sides, a channel named after the match, or a cricket programme on a cricket channel at that time), since ESPN doesn't say which channel has it.
- **Formula 1** shows each session of a race weekend as its own card: the practices, qualifying, the sprint and the race, with their times and the first three of each session once it has run.  A session's page lists the whole weekend and the drivers' championship.  Your guide is searched for each session (an F1 programme naming it at that time).
- Scores update every 30 seconds while a game is live, and only while the screen is open.  Turn the screen off in **Settings > Sports > Sports in the menu**.

**In the phone app:** a **Sports** tab with the same rows; tap a game for its score, box score, leaders, standings and the TV's channels with it, then **Watch on TV**, **Remind me** or **Follow**.

**While you watch.**  When a team you follow is playing and you're watching something else full screen (a movie, another game), its **score** sits in a corner of the screen (top right unless you pick another under **Where the scores go**), kept up to date; several take turns.  It isn't shown for the game you're watching, since the broadcast has its own.  **Alerts for my teams**: a close game near the end ("Pistons trail Raptors 102 to 105, 2:10 4th") asks whether to switch to it, wherever you are in TiviBro, and a final score says so.  **Teams in the corner** turns a team's games out of the corner, and **Final scores** turns off a team's final score message, one team at a time, while the rest stay as they are.  **Hide scores** (Settings > Sports, or a game's page) keeps a team's scores out of Sports, the corner and alerts until you show that game, for a game you recorded or will watch later.  Each is a switch in Settings > Sports.
