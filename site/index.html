Pay-per-view and sports passes

The game is in the name. Now it's in the guide.

Providers often skip the guide for pay-per-view, NFL, NBA, NHL and college passes, and write the whole listing into the channel name instead: NCAAB 001 | NORTH FLORIDA @ CHARLESTON SOUTHERN (M) | 12.18 11:00 AM | ESPN+.

  • The channel shows as NCAAB 001, with the matchup under it.
  • The guide says Starts 7:30 PM: Kings vs Pelicans, then shows the game at its real time.
  • Events by time lists every upcoming game and fight, soonest first.
  • ET, PT, UK and CET times are converted to your TV's time, daylight saving included.

The web editor

Clean up 10,000 channels from your computer, not the remote.

Turn on Edit on a computer and open the address the TV shows in any browser on your network. Changes are drafts until you press Send to TV.

  • Hide, sort, rename, copy and move channels into any group.
  • Smart clean-up: keep only your countries, pick your city for local stations, keep East or West feeds, hide duplicates, divider groups and empty groups.
  • Smart match finds the right guide for channels without one.
  • Drafts are saved on the TV within seconds, with a version history. A stray refresh never costs you an hour of work.
  • Logins never leave the TV; the editor only sees server names.

Backups

New TV? Everything back in a minute.

One file holds every playlist with its login, your groups, favorites, names, guide matches and settings, locked with your passphrase (AES-256-GCM) before it leaves the TV. On a brand-new install, choose Set up or restore from a computer on the very first screen.

Install

TiviBro installs like any homebrew app. Pick the way that suits your TV.

The easiest way on a rooted TV, with updates from the TV itself.

  1. Open the Homebrew Channel, go to Settings, and choose Add repository.
  2. Enter this address:
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ohmybahgosh/TiviBro/homebrew/repo.json
  3. TiviBro appears in the app list. Choose Install. New versions show up as Update, and inside TiviBro under Settings, Updates and about.

Switch on SSH or Telnet in the Homebrew Channel, log in as root, and run:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ohmybahgosh/TiviBro/homebrew/install.sh | sh

It downloads the newest build, checks its SHA-256, installs it and starts TiviBro. Run it again any time to update; your settings are kept.

No root? Use LG's Developer Mode and the webOS CLI.

  1. Put your TV in Developer Mode.
  2. Download the .ipk from the latest release.
  3. Install it with ares-install -d yourTV app.tivibro.tv_*.ipk, or build from source with npm run build and npm run install:tv.

TiviBro is in active development: these are test builds. Keep a backup before updating, and report bugs in Issues.

Questions

Does TiviBro come with channels?

No. TiviBro is a player. It plays your own subscription from your provider, as an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist with an XMLTV guide.

Which TVs does it run on?

LG TVs with webOS 4 (2018) and newer. It is built and tuned for webOS 25 and tested on the browser and Node engines of older webOS versions.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's MIT licensed open source, with no accounts, ads, tracking or paid tiers.

Are my logins safe?

Logins stay on the TV. The web editor only ever sees server names, it needs the password shown on the TV, and backups are always encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

Where's the full manual?

The user guide covers every remote button, every screen and every setting, with videos and screenshots.
