Pair with your TV

Scan the code in TiviBro on your TV, under Settings, Phone app, Pair a phone.

Open in the TiviBro app

No app yet? The TiviBro app for Android is coming soon. You can still edit your channels from this phone: on the TV choose Edit on phone in the menu and scan that code.

Pairing code, if the app asks:

The code stays on this phone. It is after the # in the address, which browsers never send to a website.
