TiviBro

The IPTV player LG TVs never got. A free, guide-first player for your own Xtream Codes or M3U subscription, built to feel like TiviMate and go further. TiviBro provides no channels or streams.

Features

  • Guide first: live preview, details and groups beside the guide. OK previews, OK again goes fullscreen. The Magic Remote works everywhere.
  • Set-up tour: right after you connect, keep only the countries you watch, clean channel names, pick East or West feeds and your city's local stations, and choose logos and colours. Or scan a QR code and do it on your phone.
  • Catch-up, movies and shows: rewind the guide and replay, posters, seasons, episodes, My list and resume.
  • Pay-per-view and sports passes: games written into channel names become a short name and a guide entry at the real time, plus an Events by time list.
  • Channel logos: missing logos filled in from the iptv-org and tv-logos projects.
  • Never stuck on a dead channel: switches to another version of the same channel (FHD, HD, SD) by itself.
  • A guide for every channel: channels with no guide get one from free public guides (epg.pw and iptv-epg.org).
  • Reminders, parental controls, a sleep timer and remote button remapping.
  • Web editor on a computer or phone: hide, sort, rename and copy thousands of channels, Smart clean-up, guide matching, drafts with undo, and locked backups.
  • HDR, Dolby Vision and Atmos routed to the right player.
  • Free and open source (MIT). No accounts, no ads, no tracking, just an anonymous daily install count you can switch off. Your logins never leave the TV.

Good to know

  • webOS 4 (2018) and newer, built for webOS 25.
  • Updates show up here, and inside TiviBro under Settings, Updates and about.
  • Test builds show a plain-number version here (0.5.10011 is 0.6.0 alpha 11). TiviBro itself shows the real name.
