TiviBro is in alpha testing on real LG TVs (webOS 4 to 25), with beta next. Expect some rough edges, and keep a backup (phone app or web editor, Backups and restore points) before updating.

TiviBro

The IPTV player LG TVs never got. A free, guide-first player for your own Xtream Codes, M3U or Stalker portal subscription, built to feel like TiviMate and go further. In English, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. TiviBro provides no channels or streams.

Features

  • Multiview: two channels at once, the first on a webOS IPTV app. The one you hear plays as normal video, the other as smooth live video beside it. OK swaps the sound, Blue goes fullscreen.
  • Guide first: live preview, details and groups beside the guide. OK previews, OK again goes fullscreen. The Magic Remote works everywhere.
  • Set up from your phone: scan the QR code on the first screen and paste your login there. A set-up tour keeps only the countries you watch, cleans channel names, picks East or West feeds and local stations, and chooses logos.
  • Recording, live pause and rewind: record from the guide to a USB drive (rooted TVs), pause any live channel, and a timeline in the player like TiviMate's.
  • Catch-up, movies and shows: rewind the guide and replay, posters, seasons, episodes, My list, resume and watched at 90%.
  • Sports: pay-per-view and sports passes become guide entries at the real time, an Events by time list, and My teams at the top of the guide.
  • A guide and a logo for every channel: free public guides fill in channels with none, and missing logos come from the iptv-org and tv-logos projects.
  • Never stuck on a dead channel: switches to another version of the same channel (FHD, HD, SD) by itself.
  • Web editor and phone app: hide, sort, rename and copy thousands of channels, Smart clean-up, guide matching, drafts with undo, and locked backups. The Android app is a remote and TV guide too.
  • Sixteen themes, reminders, parental controls, a sleep timer and remote button remapping.
  • HDR, Dolby Vision and Atmos routed to the right player.
  • Signed updates from Settings > System, and a way back to an earlier version if one causes trouble.
  • Free and open source (MIT). No accounts, no ads, no tracking, just an anonymous daily install count you can switch off. Your logins never leave the TV.

Good to know

  • webOS 4 (2018) and newer, built for webOS 25.
  • Updates show up here, and inside TiviBro under Settings > System.
  • Test builds show a plain-number version here (0.5.10011 is 0.6.0 alpha 11). TiviBro itself shows the real name.
