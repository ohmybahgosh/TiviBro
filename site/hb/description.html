TiviBro

The ultimate IPTV player for LG webOS. A free, guide-first player for your own Xtream Codes, M3U or Stalker portal subscription, built to feel like TiviMate and go further. TiviBro provides no channels or streams.

In 🇺🇸 English, 🇩🇪 German, 🇪🇸 🇲🇽 Spanish, 🇫🇷 French, 🇮🇹 Italian, 🇳🇱 Dutch, 🇵🇱 Polish, 🇧🇷 🇵🇹 Portuguese, 🇷🇺 Russian and 🇹🇷 Turkish.

Free here, paid elsewhere

  • A DVR: record from the guide to a USB drive (rooted TVs) or to a Windows share or WebDAV folder on your PC or NAS (any TV). TiviMate and Plex charge for it.
  • Pause and rewind any live channel for hours, with a timeline in the player, not only catch-up channels.
  • Skip intro, recap and credits, as a button or automatic. Plex keeps it for Plex Pass.
  • Dialogue boost and night mode: voices clearer over music and effects, and loud scenes brought down. Plex keeps it for Plex Pass.
  • Multiview: two channels at once, the first on a webOS IPTV app.
  • Multiple playlists, catch-up, favorites and your own groups, reminders, parental controls and locked backups: TiviMate Premium features, all included.
  • Movie and show details with no token needed: posters, plots, trailers and Up next from TheTVDB, or TMDB with your own key, chosen for movies and shows on their own like Plex's agents. Trakt too.

Features

  • Guide first: live preview, details and groups beside the guide. OK previews, OK again goes fullscreen. The Magic Remote works everywhere.
  • Set up from your phone: scan the QR code on the first screen and paste your login there. A set-up tour keeps only the countries you watch, cleans channel names, picks East or West feeds and local stations, and chooses logos.
  • Sports: today's games with live scores, each tied to the channel of yours that has it, a page per game, and your teams' scores in the corner while you watch something else. Pay-per-view and sports passes become guide entries at the real time.
  • My media: your own movies and shows from Windows and network shares, media servers, WebDAV, Google Drive or Dropbox, Plex-style, with a full-screen Up next.
  • Movies and shows from your provider: posters, seasons, episodes, trailers, My list, resume and watched at 90%.
  • A guide and a logo for every channel: free public guides fill in channels with none, and missing logos come from Worldwide TV Logos, tv-logos and iptv-org.
  • Never stuck on a dead channel: switches to another version of the same channel (FHD, HD, SD) by itself.
  • Web editor and phone app: hide, sort, rename and copy thousands of channels from a browser, with drafts and undo. The Android app is a remote and TV guide too.
  • Sound the TV can't play: DTS and Dolby TrueHD converted to Dolby Digital while you watch. HDR, Dolby Vision and Atmos routed to the right player.
  • Sixteen themes, subtitles from OpenSubtitles or your own files, a sleep timer and remote button remapping.
  • Signed updates from Settings > System, and a way back to an earlier version if one causes trouble.
  • Free and open source (MIT). No accounts, no ads, no tracking, just an anonymous daily install count you can switch off. Your logins never leave the TV.

Good to know

  • webOS 4 (2018) to webOS 26, with one install file. Works with Homebrew Channel 0.8.0.
  • Updates show up here, and inside TiviBro under Settings > System.
  • Test builds show a plain-number version here (0.5.10217 is 0.6.0 alpha 217). TiviBro itself shows the real name.
