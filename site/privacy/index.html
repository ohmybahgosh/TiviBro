Privacy

Updated November 3, 2026

TiviBro is free and open source, with no accounts and no ads. This page says everything the TV app, the phone app and this website send, and what is kept. The code is all public on GitHub if you want to check.

The TV app

  • Your provider logins, playlists, channels and settings stay on your TV. There are no TiviBro accounts or servers holding them.
  • Install numbers. Once a day TiviBro tells tivibro.app which version it is and which webOS the TV has. The first time a new version starts, TiviBro (and the install script) also say whether it was an install or an update. No ID is sent and no address is kept: each network is counted once a day using a hash that is forgotten after two days. Only the daily totals are kept. Switch it off in Settings > Updates, about and diagnostics > Count this TV in the install numbers, or run the install script with TIVIBRO_NO_COUNT=1.
  • Update checks go to GitHub (api.github.com).
  • Public guide fill-in (on by default) downloads country guides from epg.pw and iptv-epg.org (and Pluto TV's guide from i.mjh.nz when the playlist has Pluto channels) for channels nothing else covers. Nothing from your playlist is sent. Switch it off per playlist under Settings > Playlists and guide.
  • The logo library, if you turn it on, asks tivibro.app for logo lists by country only.
  • Problem reports are sent only when you choose to. Provider, guide and server names are replaced before anything leaves the TV, and the report becomes a public GitHub issue.
  • My media contacts media sources you add and their playback servers. Titles come from your files. With a saved TMDB token, movie and TV-show details are fetched automatically at startup and after scans, or when you choose Fetch details. Titles and years or saved TMDB IDs, season numbers and the preferred language are sent to api.themoviedb.org (TMDB); your saved TMDB token is used only for TMDB authentication; source logins and playback links are never sent there. Each viewer supplies their own token, which stays on their TV outside backups and problem reports and is never returned to the web editor. Poster, backdrop and episode images load from image.tmdb.org. Previously saved Cinemeta posters may still load from images.metahub.space until refreshed. Source logins stay on the TV, outside backups and problem reports. Signed playback links are not saved.
  • Dropbox, if you add it to My media, is signed in to from the phone app. Dropbox gives TiviBro read-only access to your files (it can't change or delete anything). The lasting sign-in is kept on your TV only, outside backups and problem reports, and never shown in the phone app or the web editor. The TV lists your folders and files with Dropbox and plays them from Dropbox's own short-lived links, which are kept out of the TV's log. Removing the source also asks Dropbox to end that sign-in. You can disconnect TiviBro any time in your Dropbox account's settings, under Connected apps.
  • Speed test uses Cloudflare's speed test, only when you run it.

The phone app

  • The Android app talks to your TVs on your home network, and to Dropbox only while you sign in to it there (Settings > My media > Add media > Dropbox). It sends nothing to us and has no analytics.
  • For the Dropbox sign-in, the phone opens Dropbox's own sign-in page in the browser, so your Dropbox password never reaches TiviBro. The phone swaps Dropbox's one-time code for the sign-in, hands it to the TV encrypted, and forgets it. Dropbox sends you back through tivibro.app/dropbox, a page that only opens the app and has no analytics.
  • Pairing keys are kept in the phone's secure key storage. Remove a phone on the TV and its key stops working at once.
  • Logins (a shared provider link, a password typed on the phone) and backups travel between the phone and the TV encrypted. Backups the app makes stay on the phone, encrypted, and out of Android's cloud backups.

This website

  • tivibro.app counts visits with Google Analytics only if you choose Allow on its banner. IP addresses are anonymized, and Google signals and ad personalization are off. Change your choice any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the home page.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the install counter and report relay run on Cloudflare. Like any web host, they handle your IP address to answer requests.
